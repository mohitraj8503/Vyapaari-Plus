Merchant Acquisition Strategy & Playbook

1. Overview

This playbook outlines a proposed merchant acquisition strategy for Tech Tomorrow's Vyapaari+ project. The goal is to help Indian retailers and MSMEs discover the platform, understand its benefits, and complete onboarding.

2. Target Audience

- Small and medium-sized retailers
- Kirana and neighbourhood store owners
- Local shopkeepers
- Small business owners and entrepreneurs
- Retailers looking to improve billing, inventory, and customer management

3. Value Proposition

Vyapaari+ aims to simplify everyday business operations through:

- Smart invoicing and billing
- GST compliance support
- Real-time inventory management
- Customer analytics
- AI-powered business insights

4. Merchant Acquisition Channels

Digital Channels

- Instagram and Facebook content
- LinkedIn outreach
- WhatsApp Business messages and groups
- Email campaigns
- Local business communities

Offline Channels

- Market visits and retailer meetings
- Local business associations
- Retailer referral programmes
- Demonstrations at merchant gatherings

5. 14-Day Merchant Onboarding Funnel

Days 1–3: Awareness

Introduce Vyapaari+ to retailers through social media posts, short videos, and targeted outreach.

Days 4–6: Interest

Share the key features and benefits. Use simple examples showing how digital tools can save time and organise business operations.

Days 7–9: Engagement

Contact interested merchants, answer questions, and offer a product demonstration.

Days 10–12: Conversion

Guide interested merchants through registration and onboarding. Explain the steps clearly and provide support where needed.

Days 13–14: Follow-Up

Check whether newly registered merchants need help. Encourage active usage and ask satisfied merchants to refer other retailers.

6. Merchant Outreach Message

Hello! We are introducing Vyapaari+, a business platform designed to help retailers manage billing, inventory, and customer information more efficiently.

If you are interested in exploring smarter ways to manage your shop, we would be happy to share more details and guide you through the onboarding process.

7. Hypothetical Merchant Case Studies

These examples are hypothetical and illustrate possible acquisition scenarios.

Case Study 1: Local Kirana Store

Challenge: Manual billing and difficulty tracking stock.

Approach: Demonstrate how digital billing and inventory tools could simplify daily tasks.

Case Study 2: Clothing Retailer

Challenge: Difficulty maintaining organised stock records.

Approach: Explain how digital inventory tracking can improve stock visibility.

Case Study 3: Electronics Shop

Challenge: Managing product information and customer enquiries.

Approach: Demonstrate organised product records and customer management features.

Case Study 4: Beauty and Cosmetics Store

Challenge: Tracking frequently changing inventory.

Approach: Highlight the potential benefits of keeping inventory records updated.

Case Study 5: Multi-Product Retail Shop

Challenge: Managing different product categories and sales records.

Approach: Explain how a centralised digital system can help organise business information.

8. LinkedIn Content Plan

1. Introduce the challenges faced by local retailers.
2. Explain the benefits of digital billing.
3. Share inventory management tips.
4. Discuss the importance of organised customer records.
5. Explain how technology can support small businesses.
6. Share a hypothetical merchant success scenario, clearly labelled as an example.
7. Invite retailers to learn more about Vyapaari+.

9. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Track the following metrics during the acquisition campaign:

- Number of merchants contacted
- Number of responses received
- Number of product demonstrations completed
- Number of registrations started
- Number of successful onboardings
- Merchant engagement and referrals

10. Follow-Up and Retention

- Respond promptly to merchant questions.
- Provide clear onboarding instructions.
- Check in with merchants after registration.
- Share helpful product tips.
- Collect feedback to identify areas for improvement.
- Encourage referrals without pressuring merchants.

11. Conclusion

A successful merchant acquisition strategy should focus on trust, clear communication, and practical value. By reaching retailers through relevant channels, demonstrating useful features, and providing onboarding support, Vyapaari+ can work towards building stronger relationships with Indian MSMEs and retailers.
